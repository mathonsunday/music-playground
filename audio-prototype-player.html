About these prototypes:

These audio prototypes are generated entirely in code using the Web Audio API, similar to the approach used in the Narrative OS project. No pre-recorded samples—all sounds are synthesized in real-time.

Click any prototype button to play, and switch between them anytime or stop with the same button.

Web Audio API Sound Design Capabilities:

  • Flowing Water / Streams ✓ Works great - Use bandpass filtered noise with organic wave modulation
  • Wind & Rustling Leaves ✓ Works great - Pink noise with high-pass filtering and LFO variation
  • Bird Chirps ✓ Works great - Sine oscillators with frequency envelope sweeps
  • Cricket/Insect Sounds ✓ Works well - Frequency modulation around 4-5kHz with amplitude modulation
  • Bee Buzz ✓ Possible - High frequency oscillator (600-900Hz) with fast amplitude modulation (150-200Hz)
  • Deep Drones & Pads ✓ Works very well - Sine oscillators with slow LFO modulation
  • Organic Growth Sounds ✓ Works well - Frequency sweeps (80-200Hz) with gentle amplitude envelopes
  • Detailed Crackling/Fire ⚠️ Challenging - Can create but tends to sound synthetic without noise shaping
  • Realistic Rain ⚠️ Challenging - White noise works but lacks the character of real rain impacts
  • Footsteps/Impacts ⚠️ Challenging - Difficult to make convincing without impact envelopes

The Web Audio API is excellent for sustained tones, frequency sweeps, filtered noise textures, and modulation effects. Synthetic sounds work best when they're ambient or "alive" rather than trying to replicate specific realistic impacts.
